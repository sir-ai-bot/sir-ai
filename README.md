# 🎓 Sir AI

### AI-Powered Education Platform for Bangladesh

**Education for All 🇧🇩**

---

# 📖 About

Sir AI is an AI-powered education platform built to make learning easier, smarter, and more accessible for students in Bangladesh.

The first version focuses on **Class 6 to Class 12** students based on the **NCTB curriculum**.

In the future, Sir AI aims to support **Medical, Engineering, University Admission, BCS, and Skill Development**.

---

# 🎯 Mission

To make quality education in Bangladesh easy, accessible, and AI-powered for every student.

---

# 🌍 Vision

To become Bangladesh's most trusted AI education platform and help millions of students learn from school to higher education.
